# FAO · Testing daily WaPOR data in Jendouba

FAO published this account on 3 April 2026. The pilot supplied daily Jendouba observations from January through December 2025. [FAO publication](https://www.fao.org/in-action/remote-sensing-for-water-productivity/news-and-events/news/news-detail/testing-daily-10-m-wapor-data-for-improved-irrigation-in-jendouba--tunisia/en)

> Spatial resolution was improved from 20 m to 10 m, temporal resolution from decadal to daily
